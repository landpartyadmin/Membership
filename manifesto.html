Read our manifesto

Our vision for a united and prosperous South Africa.

The Land Party will secure victory through a new democratic land revolution and found a South African people's democratic dictatorship through people's power.

Pillar One

Land

A democratic revolution in how land is owned and used in South Africa — fair redistribution that puts land back in the hands of the people who work it and live on it, ending the legacy of dispossession that still shapes the country today.

Pillar Two

People

Government that answers to the people, all the time — not just at the ballot box. People's power means ordinary South Africans have a real, ongoing say in the decisions that shape their communities.

Pillar Three

Services

Water, electricity, housing, healthcare, and education delivered reliably to every community — because a manifesto only means something when it shows up as quality of life on the ground.

The people's dictatorship we are talking about is about people's power. We are talking about taking the money back to the people. When workers earn a good living wage, the country will grow, poverty will be eradicated, and education and quality of life will improve.

Gcobani Ndzongana Chief Leader, Land Party
